⚠️ Real Phishing Example Breakdown

From: support-paypal-security@mail-secure.xyz

Subject: 🔴 Account Suspended - Action Required!

Date: Today, 9:47 AM

Dear Valued Customer,

Your account has been suspended due to unusual activity. Your identity verification is required immediately.

Click below immediately to verify your identity or your account will be permanently closed:

This action is urgent. Failure to verify within 24 hours will result in account termination.

🚩 Red Flags & Warning Signs

❌
Suspicious Domain

The email uses "mail-secure.xyz" instead of official PayPal domain (@paypal.com)

❌
Artificial Urgency

Threats of account closure force quick decisions without critical thinking

❌
Requests Credentials

Legitimate companies never ask for verification details via email links

❌
Generic Greeting

"Dear Valued Customer" instead of using your actual name

❌
Suspicious Button

Bright, urgent button designed to trigger immediate action

❌
Short Time Limit

"Within 24 hours" creates panic and bypasses rational decision-making

💡 Quick Protection Tips

1

Verify the Sender

Check the email address carefully. Look for slight misspellings or suspicious domains.

2

Hover Before Clicking

Hover over links to see the actual URL before clicking. Phishing links often redirect to malicious sites.

3

Never Share Passwords

Legitimate companies NEVER ask for passwords, PINs, or SSNs via email or links.

4

Enable Multi-Factor Auth

Add an extra security layer. Even if credentials are stolen, accounts stay protected.

5

Check for HTTPS

Look for the padlock icon and "HTTPS://" at the start. Avoid sites with "HTTP://".

6

Report Suspicious Emails

Use the "Report Phishing" or "Report Spam" button in your email provider.
